Air Traffic Management Negotiation

Late flights, solved before take-off. Three AI agents (tower, airline, ramp) agree on a plan, fixed rules from the challenge workbook check it, and the supervisor decides.

Same flight, two evenings →Animated replay: phone tree vs agents, a rule sandbox to try and break it, limits, a Changi what-if. Pitch deck →9 slides. ← → to move, N for speaker notes. Negotiation viewer →Every turn and every rule check of the recorded demo flights. Scorecard →Evaluation results over 255 flights. Monday morning at DFW →What adoption would look like for each team. Workbook deviations →The 7 limits I changed or added, and why.
Hackathon research prototype by Marlène Menoire, built solo on 31 Oct 2026. Not an operational tool. Code and results: github.com/Marl75/atm-negotiation
