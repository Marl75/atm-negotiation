    Where it stops

    What the prototype does not do yet

    Data

    Airport limits are synthetic

    Gates, runways and capacity come from the challenge workbook, scaled from traffic. Fix: one airline’s live gate plan and declared arrival rates.

    Weather

    The storm is my blind spot

    Nothing known before take-off forecasts the weather, so weather flights land later than predicted. Fix: plug in a forecast (hello, Challenge #5).

    Scale

    ≈18,000 is an estimate

    It is 5.1% of one year of challenge data, under the workbook’s limits, not a count of real incidents. Fix: shadow mode on live traffic.

    AI

    The AI varies, the rules don’t

    About 1 flight in 10 negotiates differently from one run to the next. Safety never changes: fixed rules decide. Fix: more runs, measured.

    Crew

    Crew rule simplified to 13 hours

    The real FAA table allows up to 14 hours in the morning, so some escalations are stricter than life. Fix: real crew pairings.

    People

    A human always signs off

    The tool proposes and explains. It never applies a plan, extends a crew’s duty or picks a reserve crew. That stays a person’s job.
