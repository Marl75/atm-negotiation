When there is no good answer

It says so early, instead of forcing a plan

Southwest 1347

Whatever the plan, the crew would end 10 minutes past its 13-hour limit. The airline agent refuses every option.

“No legal plan: sent to the supervisor”

Why it matters

The airline learns before take-off that it needs a fresh crew, not at the gate with passengers waiting.

≈9,000 flights in one year of challenge data had no legal plan at all (estimate).

2.6% of the 358,774 delayed flights in the 2015 data provided (rules-only audit)

Sometimes there is no good answer: here the crew would go past its 13-hour limit whatever the plan. The tool does not force anything. It tells the supervisor why, before take-off, so the airline can find a fresh crew. I estimate about nine thousand such flights in the data.
