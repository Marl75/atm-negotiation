The problem

A late flight turns into three phone calls

≈18,000

Tower: “Can I land it?”

Airline: “Do crew hours and fuel allow it?”

Ramp: “Is a gate free?”

Each team checks its own question. Nobody checks all three before landing.

5.1% of the 358,774 delayed flights in the 2015 US data provided (rules-only audit of 10,000 flights, 95% range 4.7–5.6%)

The approach supervisor at Dallas has to get three teams to agree when a flight runs late: the tower, the airline and the ramp. Today that happens by phone, and each team only checks its own rules. In one year of the challenge data, I estimate about eighteen thousand flights had a plan that broke a rule. (0:00–0:25)
